Business card layout

Drag the caricature, name, logo, QR code, or contact text to nudge its position. Scroll (or pinch, on trackpad/touch) over a piece to resize it. Arrow keys nudge position and +/- keys nudge size, both on whatever you last touched (hold Shift for bigger steps). Everything here mirrors the real card exactly, so what you see is what prints and what shows on the site. Hit Save Layout when you're happy with it -- the site updates automatically within about a minute, same as publishing from Contact info. Print / Save as PDF and the PNG/JPG downloads below use whatever's on screen right now, saved or not, so you can print a one-off without saving first.
