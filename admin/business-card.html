Print your business card

This pulls your name, photo, email, and phone straight from Contact info in /admin/ — update it there and reload this page to see the change here too. When you're ready, hit Print: it's set up to Walgreens Photo's business-card spec — a 3.625" × 2.125" page (with 1/16" bleed on every side) that trims down to the standard 3.5" × 2" card, with all text and the QR code kept safely inside the 3.375" × 1.875" safe area. Most browsers let you print directly to a compatible printer, or choose "Save as PDF" to get a file ready to upload to Walgreens (or any other print shop).

All three give you the same Walgreens-ready design — a 3.625″ × 2.125″ bleed canvas that trims to the standard 3.5″ × 2″ card. Print / Save as PDF opens your browser's print dialog: pick a printer, or choose "Save as PDF" to get a file. Download PNG or Download JPG instead saves a high-resolution (600 DPI) image straight to your computer, ready to upload wherever Walgreens Photo asks for an image file — PNG keeps the text and QR code sharpest; JPG is a smaller file if there's an upload size limit.
